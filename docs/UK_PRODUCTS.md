
## UK usual products

Open Settings → My usual products. Search UK-market Open Food Facts records by name, brand or barcode, or enter per-100-g / per-100-ml label values. Label-photo transcription fills a review form and saves nothing until confirmed. Optional fibre, sugars, saturates and salt stay unknown when absent. Source records are not guaranteed complete or current; Tesco/Asda coverage depends on contributed data. Volume records without an explicit per-100-ml basis are excluded from automatic import rather than treated as grams.

Choose Homemade on the photo note screen to use saved ingredient labels; choose Eating out / other to disable them. Saved products never add ingredients by themselves. Explicit alternate brands, fat levels and supported subtypes override defaults. Rice defaults are not automatically substituted because dry-label and cooked-photo weights differ. Each applied default is identified beside the ingredient; change foods/amounts as usual. Portion quantities remain estimates; no 90% accuracy claim is made.

Manually entered products are immutable source records. Editing a saved label creates a new record and switches the default, leaving historical meals unchanged. The legacy Item.grams field stores the amount in the source record's nutritionBasis (g by default, ml for explicit volume labels). Gram/ml changes reset the amount; implicit density conversion is prohibited. Daily extra nutrient displays show partial totals or Unknown instead of assuming missing values are zero.

## UK-only policy (24 October 2026)

New food searches, recognition, refinement, usual defaults and recipes use only UK CoFID, UK-market product records or your UK labels. USDA API calls and loading of USDA data files are disabled. Old cached US references and US-derived recipes are excluded from reuse; existing diary snapshots are retained. AI fallbacks remain labelled unverified and are not a UK database guarantee.
