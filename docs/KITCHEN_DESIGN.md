# Kitchen Notebook visual direction

Approved after review of three original Caltra interface concepts, then five Kitchen Notebook screen mockups. Designed around Caltra's existing diary, editable AI food estimates, UK product preferences and nutrient coverage.

Palette: butter yellow #f5df83, dark brown #502b1e, tomato red #b73621, paper #fff9e7. Bold DM Sans typography, monospace utility labels, ruled sections, paper surfaces and restrained offset shadows. No competitor interface, screenshots, logos or brand assets were used as design references. Colour combinations and notebook motifs are common; this is not a trademark clearance or a claim of exclusive visual originality.

Implementation uses a separate kitchen.css theme for easy review and rollback. Existing data, source disclosures, input labels, nutrient-coverage warnings and actions are preserved. Phone layouts and fictional-data workflows are exercised by tests/kitchen-browser.cjs. No decorative images substitute for user meal photos.
